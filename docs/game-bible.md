## 1. The pitch (as the notebooks describe it)

A sci-fi **first-person shooter MMO** in one shared galaxy. Players roam dense alien planets, fight creatures and robot factions, train under legendary **Masters** for signature gear, tame **pets/partners**, and drive everything from speed bikes to battle mechs. Towns are safe hubs with banks, shops and clinics; the wilds get more dangerous the farther you go. Think **RuneScape's world and progression + Skyrim's exploration + Destiny's gunplay and loot.**

The logo is already designed: a **sword and gun crossed over a setting sun**, with "ART ⚔ WAR" across it. That's the title and the brand mark.

### 2.11 Game mode idea — FPS vs Commander (added Oct 10, 2026)
Asymmetric PvP proposed by Phil. FPS players fight on the ground in first person. One or more **Commander** players see the battle from an **isometric top-down view** and control the bad guys: spawning hordes, steering swarms, ordering robot squads and dropping bosses at key moments. Natural fits from the notebooks: bug swarms (black/red ants "attack in swarm"), the Bug Master and his hybrids, XR/ZR robot squads, and Drome's army. **Update (Phil, Oct 10, later):** Phil doesn't like the arena version. The game is an **open-world MMORPG**; Commander mode is parked until it can fit the open world (e.g. world raids), not an arena.

## 4. Art style — PS1-era retro (default), with HD Stylized and Semi-Realistic render modes

As of v46, Menu → Settings → Render style switches the whole renderer: **Retro PS1** (the look below), **HD Stylized** (full resolution, cel-shaded toon lighting, sun shadows, bloom, film grade) or **Semi-Realistic** (physically based materials with bump relief, ACES tone mapping). True semi-real fidelity will eventually need real 3D models (e.g. AI-generated GLBs) rather than the procedural low-poly ones.


Low-res render with hard upscaled pixels, 15-bit dithered color, vertex snapping, affine texture warping (big surfaces tessellated so floors don't swim), Gouraud lighting, distance fog, procedural pixel textures, late-90s blue menu windows. Earlier "inked low-poly" direction retired at Phil's request.

Palette starters: Earth = military greens/tan, Mezar = honey amber + sand, Capitone = deep teal forest + ice blue, Robot City = gunmetal + neon red, Drome = black + blood red, ocean world = kelp green + bioluminescent cyan, Velocity = rust canyon orange.
